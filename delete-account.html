Account

Delete your Baruch Scribe account

Your Baruch Scribe account holds only three things: a scrambled id from your Google or Apple sign-in, your email address, and the date your free trial started. Counselee notes are never stored on our side.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Baruch Scribe and tap Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete my account, then Delete.

This removes your account from our server right away and also erases every case, note, setting and saved key on that phone.

Can't open the app?

Email the developer contact address on our Google Play or App Store listing from the email you signed in with, and ask us to delete your Baruch Scribe account. We delete it within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted: the scrambled sign-in id, your email and your trial date. We keep nothing after deletion. Files you saved to your own Google Drive or OneDrive stay in your account until you delete them there. Subscriptions are managed by Google Play or the App Store, so cancel any subscription there as well.
