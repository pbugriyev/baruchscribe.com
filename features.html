Features

Everything a session needs, nothing it doesn't

Built for one-on-one, in-person counseling first.

Live transcript, speaker by speaker

Follow the conversation as it happens, labeled You and Counselee. Tap a line to swap the label if it guessed wrong.

First-session data gathering

Know whether this is a first meeting or a follow-up, and gather the background that matters without a clipboard between you.

Halo data notes

Jot what you see as well as what you hear: posture, tears, a long pause, a change in tone. The things words alone miss.

Help me, when you need it

Stuck, distracted, or sitting with someone who isn't saying much yet? One tap gives you a few gentle next questions and an observation. Works offline.

Pre-session brief

Walk in prepared, with a short reminder of where you left off, what was assigned, and what to follow up on.

Private post-session review

Afterward, look back on what went well and what you might ask differently next time. Just for you.

Homework drafts

Start from a draft assignment shaped by the session, then edit it until it fits the person in front of you.

Structured notes

Clean, consistent session notes you review and approve. Nothing is final until you say so.

Coaching mode and practice cases

New to counseling? Practice with sample cases and get quiet coaching as you learn, at your own pace.

Patterns across sessions

See themes that keep coming back over weeks of meetings, so you can shepherd toward the heart of the matter.

Hours and supervision log

Keep track of your counseling hours and supervision notes as you work toward certification.

Private safety flags

If something concerning comes up, the app flags it for you, privately. It never reports or sends anything on its own.

Church team plans

When care involves others in your church, keep a simple shared plan of who is doing what.

Russian and Spanish
