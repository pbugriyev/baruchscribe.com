For biblical counselors

Be fully present. Let the notes take care of themselves.

Baruch Scribe is a quiet session assistant for biblical counselors, from first-timers to seasoned experts. It listens, keeps the transcript, and helps you find the next good question, so your attention stays on the person in front of you.

You lead. It listens.

Counseling is personal work. Baruch Scribe never takes the conversation from you. It stays in the background, keeping a speaker-labeled transcript, noting what you observe, and drafting notes and homework you review afterward.

The Founding 40. The first 40 counselors to join the beta get 40 days free. Help shape the app with your feedback.

A few things it does

Live transcript, speaker by speaker

Follow the conversation as it happens, labeled You and Counselee. Tap a line to swap the label if it guessed wrong.

Help me, when you need it

Stuck, distracted, or sitting with someone who isn't saying much yet? One tap gives you a few gentle next questions and an observation. Works offline.

Pre-session brief

Walk in prepared, with a short reminder of where you left off, what was assigned, and what to follow up on.

Private safety flags

If something concerning comes up, the app flags it for you, privately. It never reports or sends anything on its own.
